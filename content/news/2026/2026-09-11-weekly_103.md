## Enhancements and Fixes in 4diac FORTE


- **Build & Dependencies** 
  - Fixed CMake package configuration to correctly resolve the `open62541` dependency when OPC UA support is enabled
- **Function Blocks & Testing**
  - Added regression tests for `GEN_ADD` with multiple `REAL` inputs
    - Verified IEC 61131-3-compliant left-to-right evaluation
    - Ensured correct handling of intermediate floating-point overflow to infinity
    

## Enhancements and Fixes in 4diac Documentation

- **Build Instructions**
  - Updated the documentation for building 4diac IDE from source
- **Contribution Guidelines**
  - Corrected the IEC 61131-3 standard reference
   - Improved wording consistency in `CONTRIBUTING.md`


Many thanks to all contributors for their work and their feedback!
