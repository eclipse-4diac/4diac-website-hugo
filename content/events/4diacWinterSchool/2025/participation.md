### Participation and Registration

The 2025 edition of the 4days of Eclipse 4diac Winter School was open to up to 40 participants from industry and academia, including students in Master's and PhD programmes.

The participation fee for the full event included lunches, coffee breaks, and social events.

The following registration rates were offered:

- Regular participation (early bird): EUR 570
- Academic and student participation (early bird): EUR 420
- Late registration: EUR 700
- Single-day participation: EUR 150 (including lunch and coffee breaks, but excluding social events)

### Information for Students

The 4days of Eclipse 4diac Winter School was also designed for students in Master's and PhD programmes.

Participants who successfully completed the program could earn **3 ECTS** (European Credit Transfer and Accumulation System) credits.

Recognition of these credits at their home institution was subject to the respective institution's regulations.


### Accommodation
For the 2025 edition, discounted rooms were available at [Hotel Sommerhaus](https://www.sommerhaus-hotel.at/de/linz), located close to the Winter School venue.
