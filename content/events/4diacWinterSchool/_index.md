## About the 4days of Eclipse 4diac Winter School

The **4days of Eclipse 4diac Winter School** brings together students, researchers, developers, and industry professionals to explore IEC 61499, Eclipse 4diac, and open-source industrial automation.

Following our guiding principle of **"no theory without hands-on experience,"** the Winter School combines technical presentations with practical workshops, allowing participants to immediately apply what they have learned.

The Winter School welcomes newcomers and experienced users alike. Beyond learning how to use Eclipse 4diac, participants gain insights into its development, explore advanced concepts, and discover opportunities to contribute to the open-source project.
